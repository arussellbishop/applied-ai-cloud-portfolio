This inventory is intentionally conservative. It was created from evidence present in this repository only; absence here does not mean the qualification does not exist.

## Completed professional training

**None evidenced in this repository.** Course completion should be listed only when a completion record, provider, date and course title can be verified.

## Current / planned training

**None evidenced in this repository.** Planned study must remain separate from awarded credentials and completed training.

## Supply checklist

For each item to add later, provide:

- exact credential or course title;
- issuing organisation or training provider;
- status: awarded, completed, in progress or planned;
- award/completion date or expected date;
- public verification URL or a private record location;
- expiry/renewal date where applicable;
- permission to publish the claim.
