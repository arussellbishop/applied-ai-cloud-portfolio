# Incident 001 - synthetic model drift

## Event
A monitoring review identified a material change in attendance, performance and engagement distributions in the drift fixture.

## Containment
Pause automated recommendations, preserve the affected model/data versions, increase human review and notify the AI System Owner.

## Investigation and corrective action
Re-run data-quality, performance and subgroup tests; assess retraining, threshold change or retirement. Effectiveness is recorded in `corrective-actions/CAPA-001.md`.
