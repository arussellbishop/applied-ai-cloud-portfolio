# Internal audit plan

**Objective:** evaluate whether the fictional AIMS controls for AI-001 are implemented and producing evidence.

**Criteria:** approved AIMS scope/policy, risk and impact processes, technical evaluation, human oversight, supplier/change control, incident/CAPA and management review. Detailed normative wording is not reproduced; verify references against licensed standards.

**Method:** document review, traceability sampling, interviews, technical test observation and follow-up verification. Independence is preserved by assigning an auditor who did not own the model.
