# AI impact assessment - AI-001

## Purpose and affected people

The fictional system prioritises student-support cases for qualified staff review. Affected groups include learners, families, support staff and the education provider. Benefits may include earlier access to support; harms may include stigma, missed support, unfair prioritisation, privacy loss and automation bias.

## Foreseeable use and misuse

Foreseeable misuse includes treating a score as a final decision, using it for discipline or exclusion, expanding the purpose without review, and exposing records to unauthorised users.

## Controls and residual impact

Controls are purpose limitation, no autonomous adverse action, human review, reasons and challenge route, access control, data minimisation, subgroup testing, drift monitoring, incident response and change approval. Residual impact is **medium and not accepted for live deployment** until a competent organisation completes its legal, privacy, equality and safeguarding assessments.

## Reassessment

Reassess on model/data/supplier change, incident, complaint, drift, new population, new jurisdiction or legal/regulatory change. This structure is informed by ISO/IEC 42005:2025; verify details against the licensed standard.
