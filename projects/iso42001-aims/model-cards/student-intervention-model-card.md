# Student Intervention Predictor - model card

Synthetic logistic-regression demonstrator. It estimates a support-risk signal for triage only; it must not make a consequential decision without qualified human review. Training data and limitations are documented in the dataset card. Metrics are reproducible with the supplied script and are not evidence of production fitness.

## Governance controls
- documented purpose and prohibited uses
- threshold review and subgroup testing
- human review, contestability and escalation
- versioned change approval and monitoring
