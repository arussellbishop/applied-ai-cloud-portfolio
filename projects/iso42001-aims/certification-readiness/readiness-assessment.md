# Certification-readiness simulation

The fictional organisation has a documented AIMS scope, policy, inventory, risk process, impact assessment, technical evidence, audit, CAPA and management review. Readiness is **READY WITH OPEN ACTIONS** for a simulated external review, not certified.

Open actions are CAPA-001, verification of exact normative references against licensed standards, completion of organisation-specific legal/privacy approvals, and independent auditor review. A certification body would determine conformity under its own accredited process.
