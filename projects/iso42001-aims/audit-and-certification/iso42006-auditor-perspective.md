# ISO/IEC 42006 auditor perspective

ISO/IEC 42006:2025 addresses requirements for bodies providing audit and certification of an AI management system. It is therefore relevant to understanding the certification ecosystem, not a control set that EduAssist AI Ltd implements as a client. This repository simulates client readiness and internal audit only; it is not a certification body and makes no accreditation claim. Verify scope and normative details against a licensed copy.
