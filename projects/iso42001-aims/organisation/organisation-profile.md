# EduAssist AI Ltd - Synthetic Organisation Profile

EduAssist AI Ltd is a fictional UK-headquartered education-technology organisation with approximately 180 employees and selected UK/EU customers. It develops and deploys AI-assisted student-support tools. Educators remain responsible for significant decisions.

## Context

Internal issues include rapid model change, limited specialist assurance capacity, inconsistent data quality and pressure to demonstrate measurable student-support outcomes. External issues include privacy expectations, equality risks, supplier dependency, regulatory change and customer demand for explainable systems.

## Interested parties

Students, parents/guardians, educators, safeguarding leads, customers, regulators, suppliers, employees, senior management and affected communities.

## Risk appetite

No tolerance for unauthorised disclosure, covert automated consequential decisions, unreviewed high-impact deployment or deliberate discrimination. Moderate tolerance for controlled experimentation using synthetic or minimised data with documented approval.
