# AIMS Scope

**Organisation:** EduAssist AI Ltd (synthetic)

**Scope:** Governance, development, acquisition, deployment, operation, monitoring, change and retirement of AI systems used to support education-related services in the UK and selected EU customer contexts.

**Included:** AI inventory, use-case approval, data governance, model lifecycle, impact and risk assessment, human oversight, supplier management, security, privacy, technical evaluation, incident response, audit and continual improvement.

**Excluded:** Physical facilities, general HR operations not involving AI, unrelated finance systems, and non-AI product functionality. These exclusions do not remove legal or contractual obligations where an included AI system depends on them.

**Interfaces:** Information security management, privacy management, procurement, safeguarding, quality management and customer support.

**Objective:** Demonstrate a repeatable evidence-based AIMS process for responsible AI development and use.
