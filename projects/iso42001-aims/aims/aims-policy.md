# Synthetic AI Management Policy

EduAssist AI Ltd will develop, acquire and use AI only where the purpose, risks, affected people, controls and human accountability are understood and documented.

The organisation will maintain an AI inventory, assess impacts and risks before deployment, govern data and suppliers, test performance and fairness, provide meaningful human oversight, protect personal and confidential information, monitor operation, manage change and improve controls when evidence shows a weakness.

AI outputs are not authoritative decisions by themselves. Consequential decisions require authorised human review and a route for challenge and correction.

The accountable owner will provide resources, review objectives and risk, and ensure that staff are competent. The DPO/privacy lead, security lead and assurance lead may require escalation or suspension where risk is not controlled.
