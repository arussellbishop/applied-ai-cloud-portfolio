# AI Governance Charter

## Decision rights

- Board/senior management: approves scope, risk appetite, resources and residual-risk acceptance.
- AI accountable owner: owns the AIMS and portfolio of AI systems.
- System owner: owns purpose, operation, monitoring and user outcomes.
- DPO/privacy lead: advises on personal-data processing, rights and DPIA requirements.
- Security lead: owns threat modelling, access, incident and technical security assurance.
- Assurance lead: maintains evidence, audit programme, findings and follow-up.
- Domain reviewer: challenges outputs and approves consequential use.

## Escalation

Any person may escalate suspected harm, discrimination, security weakness, privacy breach, unsafe output or uncontrolled model change. The system owner may pause use; senior management decides restart after evidence review.
