# Free online reference library

This library contains public links and original summaries only. It does not reproduce copyrighted ISO standards. ISO Online Browsing Platform material is a preview/reference aid, not a substitute for licensed access to the complete standard.

## Standards and terminology

- [ISO/IEC 42001 official page](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001) - official scope, edition, publication status and preview for the AI management-system standard.
- [ISO/IEC 42005 official page](https://www.iso.org/standard/42005?browse=ics) - official scope and preview for AI system impact assessment.
- [ISO/IEC 23894 official page](https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html) - official scope and preview for AI risk-management guidance.
- [ISO/IEC 5338 official page](https://www.iso.org/standard/81118.html) - official scope and preview for AI system lifecycle processes.
- [ISO AI standards catalogue](https://www.iso.org/sectors/it-technologies/ai) - public catalogue for status, editions and related JTC 1/SC 42 work.
- [BSI Knowledge](https://knowledge.bsigroup.com/) - legitimate UK access, purchase and institutional subscription route for BS/EN/ISO standards.

## NIST

- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) - voluntary AI risk-management framework organised around Govern, Map, Measure and Manage.
- [NIST AI RMF resources](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework/ai-risk-management-framework-resources) - official AI RMF 1.0, Playbook, Generative AI Profile, crosswalks and related materials.
- [NIST AI RMF Playbook](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/) - free suggested actions and downloadable formats. It is guidance, not a certification checklist.
- [NIST Generative AI Profile](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/genai/) - voluntary risk guidance for generative AI use cases.

## UK government and assurance

- [UK Government AI Playbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-playbook-for-the-uk-government) - public-sector guidance on responsible, secure and controlled AI adoption.
- [Introduction to AI assurance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/introduction-to-ai-assurance) - practical overview of assurance techniques and lifecycle use.
- [Portfolio of AI assurance techniques](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/portfolio-of-ai-assurance-techniques) - public examples of impact assessment, bias audit, compliance audit, performance testing and other methods.
- [Interactive AI assurance techniques](https://www.gov.uk/ai-assurance-techniques) - searchable public catalogue of assurance case studies.
- [UK AI Standards Hub](https://www.turing.ac.uk/research/interest-groups/ai-standards-hub) - public standards-navigation, research and participation resources.
- [Government Data and AI Ethics Framework](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/data-ethics-framework/data-and-ai-ethics-framework) - public principles and practical governance guidance.

## UK privacy, law and regulation

- [ICO AI and data protection guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/) - regulatory guidance on lawfulness, fairness, transparency, accountability, security and individual rights.
- [ICO AI and data protection risk toolkit](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/guidance-on-ai-and-data-protection/ai-and-data-protection-risk-toolkit/) - practical risk and control support; the ICO marks it as under review following legislative change.
- [UK legislation](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/) - official source for UK Acts and revised statutory text.
- [Data Protection Act 2018](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12/contents) - official legislation source.
- [Equality Act 2010](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/15/contents) - official legislation source relevant to equality and discrimination analysis.

## EU and international law/policy

- [EU AI Act, EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) - authoritative EU regulation text and versions.
- [European Commission AI Act standardisation](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/european-standards/harmonised-standards/artificial-intelligence_en) - public information on harmonised-standard development; drafts must not be treated as final law.
- [EDPB](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents_en) - official European data-protection board opinions, guidelines and recommendations.
- [OECD AI principles](https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles) - international policy principles and related tools; voluntary, not legislation.

## Security and technical assurance

- [NCSC secure AI guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/machine-learning) - UK cyber-security guidance for machine-learning systems.
- [OWASP GenAI Security Project](https://genai.owasp.org/) - open security guidance and risk taxonomies for LLM and generative-AI systems.
- [MITRE ATLAS](https://atlas.mitre.org/) - public knowledge base of adversarial tactics and techniques against AI-enabled systems.
- [Fairlearn](https://fairlearn.org/) - open-source fairness assessment tooling; tool output requires contextual interpretation.
- [scikit-learn](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/) - open-source machine-learning library used for the synthetic technical demonstrator.

## How to use these sources

Record the source URL, retrieval date, version/status and authority type in the project registers. Treat law, regulator guidance, government guidance, voluntary frameworks, internal policy and analyst recommendations as separate categories. Recheck current status before relying on any source in professional advice, an audit or an examination answer.
