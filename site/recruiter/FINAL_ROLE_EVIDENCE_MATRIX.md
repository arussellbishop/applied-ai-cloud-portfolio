## Role targeting

- AI Engineer: lead with AIVouch application/API validation, then qualified historical ML work; do not claim model-quality evaluation or production LLM impact.
- AI Platform / Infrastructure / MLOps Engineer: lead with reproducible container validation, release controls and recovery; distinguish missing training/model-registry operations.
- Platform / DevOps / Cloud Engineer: lead with implemented AWS delivery, security boundaries and CI/IaC.
- Solutions / Cloud / AI Architect: explain trade-offs, cloud-specific adapters and cost/security decisions; do not imply deployed multi-cloud scale.
- Senior applied-AI/platform roles: use the evidence to discuss judgement, and establish leadership/scope separately from this portfolio.
