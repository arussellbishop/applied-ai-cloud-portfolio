Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Russell AI OS uses information when the owner authorises the application to connect to Google services for personal productivity, research and job-search workflows.

Information accessed

When authorised, Russell AI OS may access limited information from Google Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive. Access is restricted to the scopes displayed on the Google consent screen. The application is designed to use read-only access for monitoring, indexing and retrieval unless a separate, clearly identified action scope is explicitly authorised.

How information is used

Information is used to produce personal summaries, reminders, document indexes, search results, backup manifests and retrieval-augmented research context. It is not used for advertising, profiling other people, selling data or training a public model.

Storage and processing

Authorised data is processed by the owner’s Ubuntu computer and, where the owner has configured it, stored in the owner’s Google Drive repository for backup and recovery. Local indexes and generated summaries are kept under the owner’s control. The application does not intentionally transfer Google data to unrelated third parties.

Sharing

Russell AI OS does not sell or share Google user data with advertisers or data brokers. The owner may explicitly choose to send an application email, publish a portfolio item, or share a document; those actions are separate from automatic monitoring and require the relevant user-authorised workflow.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls retention of local files, Drive files, indexes and backups. The owner can delete stored copies or revoke Google access at any time from the Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents future API access but does not automatically delete copies already retained by the owner.

Security

OAuth tokens are stored locally with restricted file permissions. The system uses least-privilege scopes and records connector status without recording token values. No security method is guaranteed to be perfect, so the owner should review connected applications and local backups periodically.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the application’s data practices change. Questions about this personal application can be directed to the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
