# Quantitative Intelligence — research discipline before performance

**Status: PARTIALLY_PROVEN historical research evidence; no investment-return or deployable-trading claim.**

## Problem and method

Investigate interpretable market regimes while separating exploratory results from evidence that can survive temporal, provenance and baseline checks. The [existing research dossier](https://github.com/arussellbishop/ai-ml-aws-portfolio/blob/a2f1e34b1b06650640d895c36d993b973b41c169/site/quant-research.html) records HMM work on 857 weekly observations, train-only preprocessing and prefix-only inference. Historical reconciliation reports two clean replays, but this audit did not rerun the original private inputs. The record explicitly does not establish HMM outperformance over gold buy-and-hold on a comparable window.

Finance Lab records experiment/rejection registries, Monte Carlo diagnostics, Benjamini–Hochberg multiple-testing controls and cost assumptions. Its final matrix reports no pre-holdout eligible candidate and an untouched holdout. Reporting a rejected hypothesis is useful engineering evidence; it is not a hidden successful strategy.

## Evaluation and provenance boundaries

Train-only transforms and prefix inference are documented no-lookahead controls for that study, not a guarantee across every project. Walk-forward evaluation is a required design discipline; a fully reproducible walk-forward pipeline is not established by the accessible evidence. Point-in-time availability, release calendars and immutable corrections must be reconciled before making a leakage-free claim. Separate later research-continuous observations must not be merged into frozen dissertation results.

The source-input-only rebuild report was INVALID because required raw inputs and regeneration code were absent from the reviewed root. Date inconsistencies, stale/failed sources and an unreconciled aggregate history remained. No approved live portfolio or execution path is established; no licensed/proprietary dataset is republished. [Research pipeline diagram](https://github.com/arussellbishop/applied-ai-cloud-portfolio/blob/main/architecture/diagrams/README.md#quantitative-research)

## Result and next improvement

The strongest result is explicit research governance: frozen questions/inputs, temporal controls, like-for-like baselines, rejection records and honest negative-result reporting. A future isolated replay would require licensed frozen inputs, reconstructed lineage, predefined walk-forward splits and fresh report hashes before revisiting performance claims.
