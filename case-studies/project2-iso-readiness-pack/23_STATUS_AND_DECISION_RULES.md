# Requirement and Evidence Decision Rules

## Requirement status

- `OPEN`: not assessed or evidence incomplete;
- `EVIDENCE_REQUESTED`: client evidence requested;
- `PARTIALLY_IMPLEMENTED`: some implementation or evidence exists;
- `IMPLEMENTED`: control is implemented but effectiveness is not yet demonstrated;
- `EFFECTIVE`: implementation and effectiveness evidence were reviewed by a competent human;
- `NONCONFORMITY`: requirement or agreed control is not met;
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`: documented reason approved by the accountable owner;
- `SUPERSEDED`: replaced by a newer requirement or scope decision.

## Evidence rules

Evidence must be relevant to the defined scope, attributable, dated, versioned, protected against inappropriate alteration and reviewed by a competent person.

A blank template, model response, policy promise or unverified assertion is not evidence of effective implementation.

## Conclusion rules

- Any critical unresolved nonconformity prevents a conforming conclusion.
- Missing evidence is reported as a gap, not silently treated as conformity.
- Analyst interpretation is labelled separately from source authority.
- Legal conclusions require appropriate legal review.
- Certification claims require an independent certification body.
