# Internal Audit Plan

## Before audit

- freeze the assessment scope and as-of date;
- confirm applicable law, guidance and selected frameworks;
- sample systems, risks, controls and evidence;
- confirm auditor independence from the work being audited;
- define sampling and interview plan.

## Audit workpapers

Record criterion, evidence inspected, interviewee, sample, finding, severity, objective evidence and conclusion. Do not convert absence of evidence into an assumed pass.

## Findings

Classify finding as conformity, observation, minor nonconformity, major nonconformity or opportunity for improvement according to the approved audit method. Each finding needs an owner, correction, root cause, due date and effectiveness check.

## Output

Issue an audit report to the accountable owner and retain workpapers in the evidence register.
