# Scope, Context and Objectives

## Assessment scope

Complete before assessment:

- organisation and business unit;
- AI system and use case;
- locations, users and interfaces;
- data types and jurisdictions;
- lifecycle stages in scope;
- suppliers and dependencies;
- exclusions and justification;
- assessment date and target standard(s).

## Interested parties

Record affected people, users, staff, customers, regulators, suppliers, owners, auditors and senior management. For each, record needs, obligations, communication route and evidence.

## AI system boundary

Document model, prompts, retrieval corpus, embedding model, vector index, application, operating system, access controls, logs, human review and downstream decisions.

## Objectives

Objectives must be measurable, owned and dated. Examples:

- 100% of production use cases have an approved inventory record;
- 100% of high-risk outputs receive human review;
- 100% of substantive RAG answers expose validated source identifiers;
- all critical incidents are triaged within the defined response time;
- all source changes affecting assessments receive review.
