# Boundaries and Limitations

This pack supports preparation and evidence management for AI governance and assurance. It does not reproduce ISO standards, provide legal advice, guarantee compliance or issue certification.

The RAG is an evidence assistant. It can retrieve and organise source material, but human reviewers remain responsible for legal interpretation, applicability decisions, risk acceptance, control effectiveness and final reports.

Encryption is not required for synthetic build fixtures. It is mandatory before real client or personal data is stored on the USB.
