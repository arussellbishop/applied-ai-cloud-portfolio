# Assurance Levels and Independence

Use proportionate assurance. Record which level was performed and why.

## Level 1: Operational self-control

The system owner performs checks, records evidence and manages day-to-day risk.

## Level 2: Management or independent internal review

An assessor not responsible for the control challenges scope, evidence, testing and conclusions.

## Level 3: Independent external assurance or certification

An appropriately competent external party assesses the defined scope against agreed criteria. Certification, where applicable, is issued only by an authorised independent certification body.

The workstation supports all three levels administratively, but it does not create independence and cannot issue certification.
