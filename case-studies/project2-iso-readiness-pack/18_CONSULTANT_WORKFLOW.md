# Consultant Engagement Workflow

## Stage 1: Intake and independence

Confirm client, engagement purpose, scope, authority to access evidence, confidentiality, conflicts, deliverables, assumptions, exclusions, timetable and escalation contact.

## Stage 2: Discovery

Interview accountable owner, system owner, DPO/privacy lead, security lead, procurement, HR/people lead and operational users as relevant. Build the AI inventory, data flow, supplier map and system boundary.

## Stage 3: Criteria and legal analysis

Agree jurisdiction, assessment date, selected standards, legal obligations, regulatory guidance, government guidance and voluntary frameworks. Record each applicability decision and obtain competent legal review where interpretation is material.

## Stage 4: Assessment

Assess context, leadership, planning, risk, data, lifecycle, human oversight, security, privacy, fairness, transparency, suppliers, competence, operations, monitoring and improvement. Link every conclusion to evidence or mark it as a gap.

## Stage 5: Testing and audit

Run the agreed technical, governance and human-review tests. Preserve failed results. Perform independent review or internal audit with workpapers and findings.

## Stage 6: Reporting and closeout

Issue a report containing scope, criteria, evidence, findings, risk treatment, limitations, confidence and conclusion. Obtain client approval. Track corrective actions and schedule follow-up review.

## Consultant rule

The consultant facilitates evidence-based assessment. The client retains accountability for legal decisions, risk acceptance, implementation and management-system operation.
