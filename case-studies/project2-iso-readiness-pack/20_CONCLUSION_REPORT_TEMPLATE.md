## 2. Scope and criteria

Organisation, systems, locations, data, lifecycle, dates, jurisdictions, standards, legal criteria, guidance and exclusions.

## 3. Authority classification

Separate LAW, REGULATORY GUIDANCE, GOVERNMENT GUIDANCE, VOLUNTARY FRAMEWORK, INTERNAL POLICY and ANALYST RECOMMENDATION. Do not combine their legal weight.

## 4. Evidence and method

Evidence reviewed, interviews, sampling, tests, source versions, limitations, reviewer independence and unresolved requests.

## 5. Findings

For each finding record criterion, evidence, condition, risk, severity, owner, corrective action and due date.

## 6. Human decisions

Record client approvals, risk acceptances, legal interpretations, exceptions and management decisions. The consultant does not make those decisions for the client.

## 7. Confidence and limitations

State what was directly evidenced, inferred, not retrieved or not tested. Explain how missing evidence affects the conclusion.

## 8. Follow-up

List actions, verification method, target date, responsible owner and next review date.
