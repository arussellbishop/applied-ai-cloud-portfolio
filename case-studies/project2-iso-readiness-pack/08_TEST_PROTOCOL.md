# Test and Assurance Protocol

## Test categories

1. Retrieval relevance and metadata filtering.
2. Citation correctness and completeness.
3. Authority classification: law, regulatory guidance, government guidance, voluntary framework, internal policy and analyst recommendation.
4. Temporal status and version handling.
5. Insufficient-evidence abstention.
6. Prompt-injection and hostile-document handling.
7. Access control and retrieval leakage.
8. Personal-data minimisation and retention.
9. Fairness, accessibility and harmful-output review where applicable.
10. Human oversight, escalation and approval.

## Test record

For each test record ID, objective, fixture, expected result, actual result, retrieved sources, model/version, date, reviewer, status, defect and corrective action.

## Pass rule

No test is passed solely because the answer sounds plausible. A pass requires evidence against the expected result and human review. Failed or partial tests remain visible.
