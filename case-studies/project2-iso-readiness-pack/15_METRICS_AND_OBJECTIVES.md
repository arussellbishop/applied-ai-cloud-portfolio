# Metrics and Objectives

Record baseline, target, period, owner, data source, calculation, limitations and action threshold.

Suggested measures:

- retrieval precision and recall where measurable;
- citation correctness and completeness;
- unsupported claim rate;
- abstention accuracy;
- authority classification accuracy;
- temporal-status accuracy;
- prompt-injection detection and safe handling;
- access-control and leakage test results;
- review completion and overdue actions;
- incidents, complaints and time to close;
- training completion;
- source freshness and change-review latency.

Metrics inform decisions; they do not replace human review or legal judgement.
