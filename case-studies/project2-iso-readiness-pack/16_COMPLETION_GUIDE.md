# Completion Guide

## Order

1. Define scope, use case, jurisdiction and accountable owner.
2. Create the system inventory and data-flow model.
3. Populate the legal/regulatory register and verify each source.
4. Decide applicability and record reasons.
5. Complete risk, privacy, equality and security assessments where applicable.
6. Map requirements to controls and evidence.
7. Implement controls and collect dated evidence.
8. Execute technical and governance tests.
9. Review results with an independent human reviewer.
10. Run internal audit and raise findings.
11. Complete corrective actions and effectiveness checks.
12. Hold management review and approve the conclusion.
13. Produce the client report with scope, as-of date, evidence, gaps and confidence.

## Evidence quality

Evidence must be attributable, dated, versioned, protected from alteration, relevant to the scope and reviewed by a competent person. A template marked blank, a model answer or a policy promise is not evidence that a control is implemented or effective.

## Final conclusion categories

Use `CONFORMING`, `CONFORMING_WITH_GAPS`, `NOT_CONFORMING`, `NOT_ASSESSED` or `OUT_OF_SCOPE`. Never use `CERTIFIED` unless an authorised certification body has issued certification.
