# Incident, Change and Retention Procedure

## Incident

Identify, contain, preserve evidence, assess impact, notify required parties, recover, conduct root-cause review and track corrective action. Examples include data leakage, prompt injection, poisoned documents, unauthorised access, harmful output and source-status error.

## Change

Every model, prompt policy, retrieval algorithm, source, embedding model, index, access rule or deployment change requires impact assessment, approval, test evidence, rollback plan and post-change review.

## Retention

Define retention by record type, legal/business need, access role, deletion method, legal hold and review date. Do not retain prompts or outputs by default without a documented purpose.
