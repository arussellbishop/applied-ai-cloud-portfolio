# Risk and Impact Method

## Rating

Rate likelihood and impact from 1 to 5. Record inherent score, existing controls, residual score, treatment decision, owner, due date and acceptance authority.

Impact must consider individuals, groups, rights, safety, service access, confidentiality, integrity, availability, equality, environment, organisation and legal/regulatory consequences.

## Treatment

Choose: avoid, reduce, transfer/share, accept or discontinue. Risk acceptance must be explicit, time-limited and approved by an authorised person.

## AI-specific analysis

Assess data quality, bias, explainability, human oversight, misuse, prompt injection, data leakage, retrieval leakage, model supply chain, provider dependency, drift, harmful output, over-reliance and incident response.

## Required records

Keep system inventory, data flow, threat model, DPIA or privacy assessment where applicable, equality/fairness assessment, test results, control evidence, approvals and review dates.
