# Synthetic Case Study: Sixth-Form AI Tutoring Assistant

**Classification: SYNTHETIC / NOT CLIENT DATA / NOT A REAL ENGAGEMENT**

This case study demonstrates the governance and assurance workflow for a hypothetical AI tutoring assistant used by a sixth-form or FE provider.

## Scenario

The assistant answers curriculum questions using an approved teaching corpus. It does not make admissions, disciplinary, grading or safeguarding decisions. A teacher remains responsible for learning outcomes and any consequential action.

## In scope

- synthetic student questions and assistant answers;
- retrieval from an approved local corpus;
- hallucination, bias, privacy and prompt-injection risks;
- staff training, human oversight and escalation;
- evidence-linked governance records.

## Out of scope

- real students, staff, schools or suppliers;
- real personal data;
- production deployment;
- legal advice or certification;
- claims about a completed client engagement.
