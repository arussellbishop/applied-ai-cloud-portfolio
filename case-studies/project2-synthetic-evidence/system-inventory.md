## Data flow

1. A synthetic question is submitted locally.
2. Retrieval searches the approved local corpus.
3. Retrieved passages are passed to the local model as untrusted evidence.
4. The model produces a draft answer with source identifiers.
5. A human reviews any consequential use.
6. Synthetic test results are stored in the assurance register.
